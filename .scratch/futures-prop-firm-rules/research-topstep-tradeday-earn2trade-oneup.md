## Due-diligence questions requiring written confirmation

1. Ask **Topstep/TradeDay/Earn2Trade** to confirm the exact NQ and MNQ contract identifiers, platform/data-feed exceptions, and whether the answer applies to evaluation, simulated funded, and live stages.
2. Ask TradeDay for a product-stage-specific Fast Pass payout schedule and its current permitted-products list.
3. Ask Earn2Trade for the current detailed withdrawal-policy URL/text (including KYC/tax/payment method and caps).
4. Ask every firm to confirm eligibility, payout availability and tax/KYC documentation for the relevant residency; this research makes **no** residency eligibility assumption.
